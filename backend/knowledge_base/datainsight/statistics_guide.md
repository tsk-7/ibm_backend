# DataInsight Statistics Guide

DataInsight summarizes numerical columns with non-missing count, mean, median, sample standard deviation, minimum, maximum, first quartile, and third quartile. Categorical columns are summarized with non-missing count, number of unique values, and most frequent value/count.

These are descriptive statistics for the current processed dataset. Interpret them alongside missingness, data types, units, distribution shape, and any previous preprocessing recorded in history.
