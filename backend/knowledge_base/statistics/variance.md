# Variance and Standard Deviation

Variance measures squared deviation from the mean. Sample variance divides the sum of squared deviations by n-1; population variance divides by n. Standard deviation is the square root of variance and is expressed in the original measurement units.

These measures are sensitive to extreme observations and are most interpretable with numeric data. Always identify whether a statistic is sample or population when that distinction matters. DataInsight reports sample standard deviation for its numeric summaries.
