# Mean, Median, and Mode

The arithmetic mean is the sum of numeric observations divided by their count. The median is the middle ordered observation (or the midpoint of the two middle observations). The mode is the most frequent value and can be used with categorical data.

The mean uses every magnitude but is sensitive to extreme values. The median is more resistant to extremes and is often a useful center for skewed data.
