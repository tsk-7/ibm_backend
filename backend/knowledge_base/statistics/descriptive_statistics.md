# Descriptive Statistics

Descriptive statistics summarize observed data. Count indicates non-missing observations; mean and median describe center; standard deviation describes spread around the mean; minimum and maximum show endpoints; and Q1/Q3 describe the central distribution.

Always interpret summaries alongside missingness, units, sample size, and shape. Mean and standard deviation can be strongly affected by skew and extremes; the median and quartiles are more robust. A summary describes the observed sample and does not by itself establish causality or representativeness.
