# Correlation

Correlation measures association between two variables. Pearson correlation describes linear association for numeric variables and ranges from -1 to 1. A value near zero does not rule out nonlinear association, and an association does not establish causation.

Inspect a scatter plot, sample size, missingness, and potential influential observations. Correlations can be misleading when groups, time trends, or confounders are present. Do not infer a causal mechanism from correlation alone.
